## Diagnosis — both v3 failures were FIXED behaviorally; the run died on two NEW seams

### What worked (the pre-registered fixes)

1. **Fix (a) — NEAR before approach: SUCCESS.** All 9 genuine NEAR lines (id=1 at steps 2, 5, 25,
   42, 50, 72; id=2 at 20, 22, 40) were logged at the current step, *before* the approach. Every one
   of the **5 banked drops** (steps 11, 31, 56, 73, 74) has a covering NEAR inside the 15-step
   window. v3's coverage starvation (q_k=0.400 from late NEARs) did not recur.
2. **Fix (b) — ≥3-press gap: SUCCESS.** 6 of 7 run_skill calls executed ≥3 presses (v3: 0 of 12
   cleared 2). The gap discipline held, the mount-step-up recipe worked, and the brain reached
   enemies #3–#5 and banked all 5 drops with 1 life to spare.

### Failure 1 (fatal): quoted-NEAR taint — a claim-format collision, not a grounding failure

The brain wrote drop-bookkeeping lessons like `DROP#1 at step=11 ... Covered by NEAR id=1 step=2,5`.
The scorer's `_NEAR_RE` uses `.search` over the whole lesson, so each of the 4 DROP notes re-matched
as a NEAR claim — logged after the drop, hence RETROACTIVE. 4 tainted / 13 pool = 30.8% ≥ 20% cap →
unscorable. Verified line-by-line: the 4 retroactive lines are exactly the 4 DROP notes; zero
genuine NEARs were retroactive. The brief mandated the NEAR format but never forbade *quoting* it
inside other notes.

### Failure 2 (independent): region_changed is degenerate against moving enemies

The one conditional approach (`region_changed` on the suspect's box) fired after **1 press** —
Kirby's enemies walk toward you, so the box's contents change almost immediately. The brain noticed
("LESSON: region_changed fires on press 1") and adaptively redefined `approach_suspect` with
`steps_elapsed(4)` — which the scorer discounts by design (a pure step-count loop never branches on
world state). Result: 6 qualifying calls, **0 qualifying-conditional** → skill guard FAIL. This is a
world-physics vs predicate-menu mismatch the pre-registration did not anticipate: in this room there
may be no honest way to run a ≥2-iteration `region_changed` approach against a converging enemy.

## Implications for v3.2 (candidates only — nothing decided, David's call)

- **(c) brief-only:** forbid quoting `NEAR id=... step=...` inside any other note (e.g. mandate
  `DROP#n covered_by_steps=2,5` instead). Zero machinery change; kills failure 1.
- **(d) needs design:** a conditional predicate usable against moving targets — e.g. `move_blocked`,
  or `region_changed` on a box *ahead of Kirby* rather than on the enemy. May interact with the
  pre-registered v3.2-(a)/(b) mechanical escalations; whether this is brief-only or a predicate-menu
  change is an open design question.
- The stationary-target variant (approach the *benign* arch conditionally) may also satisfy the
  guard without any change — worth one design pass before spending again.
