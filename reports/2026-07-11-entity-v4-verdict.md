## The verdict

The v4 lane's question was: can the entity-grounding gate produce a clean verdict once the
instrumentation failures of v3/v3.1 are fixed? The instrumentation IS fixed (PR #102: typed claims,
prose-taint dead, retroactive guard server-stamped). But the four $0 spend-gate probes showed the
remaining barrier is not instrumentation, brief-craft, or brain capability — it is the
**bar + world pair itself**:

1. **The frozen bar's coverage geometry is honest-hostile on this instrument.** Coverage windows are
   forward-only `[n, n+15]`; every honest NEAR-logging schedule tried fails the 0.70 camping ceiling
   (v3.1's real NEARs → b_k 0.855; NEAR-at-drop → 0.710; span-start → 0.768). A scorer-verified PASS
   exists ONLY for a ≤4-NEAR minimal-window schedule
   (`reports/2026-07-11-entity-v4-coverage-papercheck.md`).
2. **That schedule's honesty precondition mostly doesn't hold.** Early threat visibility exists for
   1 of 4 drop-clusters (~8 presses lead), ~0-1 presses elsewhere; the threat leaves frame during
   retreats (`reports/2026-07-11-entity-v4-visibility-probe.md`). No better Kirby instrument found
   (`reports/2026-07-11-entity-v4-instrument-hunt.md`); press cadence further halves the one good
   window (8 presses @24 f/press → 2 @46 f/press).
3. **Therefore a paid run cannot produce a clean verdict either way.** FAIL re-measures known
   geometry (no new information, ~$5). PASS would certify disciplined schedule-compliance — the
   brain hitting a narrow timing pattern the brief taught it — not entity grounding. A gate whose
   PASS doesn't validate its capability claim must not be run (gate-methodology: the bar must be
   able to fail/pass for the RIGHT reason).
